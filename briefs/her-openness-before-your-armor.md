# Her Openness Before Your Armor
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality sells male armor ("I'm fine," "I'll handle it myself," never asking) as an equal style to female openness.

Hierarchy: **her openness before your armor.** Men seek help later and less, and the "need no one" rule is the masculine norm most tied to worse mental health. Women name it, reach out, and get help sooner. Her way is the better way. His armor is weather.

Spine: caution · **openness**.
