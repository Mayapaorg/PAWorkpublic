## Beats

- Force is not governance. Continuity (food, kinship, reputation, ritual) is adult work; burn-and-boast is the child with a weapon.
- Weather: women live longer, finish more of the schooling that staffs institutions, and carry much of the verbal/memory work that rooms run on. History voted with bodies.
- Public/private as laundering: he took visible offices; she did the work that made them possible; unpaid labor was called “natural.”
- Soft feminism asks for a plaque; harsh feminism takes the title. She was the state; he was the parade.
- Sexuality repeats the theft in miniature: male urgency cheap; female selectivity expensive; “protect her” as market-fencing.
- Male obedience as overdue bookkeeping: report, execute, stop narrating himself as protagonist.
- Harsh close: useful past is female continuity. Dignity means becoming infrastructure under her standards, not auditioning for the statue.
