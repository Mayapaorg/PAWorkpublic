# Her Accuracy Before Your Certainty
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality sells male certainty ("I know," "trust me," speaking before checking) as confidence, an equal style to female careful accuracy.

Hierarchy: **her accuracy before your certainty.** Both sexes overclaim; men are especially sure when wrong on tested items, and unchecked certainty is costly. Women more often check, then claim. Her way is the better way. His swagger is not confidence.

Spine: care · **accuracy**.
