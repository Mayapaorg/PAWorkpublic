# Her Care Before Your Neglect
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality sells male toughing-it-out ("it'll pass," "it's nothing," skipping the doctor, self-medicating) as stoicism, an equal style to female health care-seeking.

Hierarchy: **her care before your neglect.** Men consult far less in the working years, delay when they're ill, and die more often of what could have been caught. Women notice early, get checked, and follow through. Her way is the better way. His neglect is not toughness.

Spine: patience · **care**.
