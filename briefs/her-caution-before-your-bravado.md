# Her Caution Before Your Bravado
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality sells male bravado (the "trust me," the fast bold bet) as an equal style to female carefulness.

Hierarchy: **her caution before your bravado.** Men trade more and keep less, and are surest when wrong. Her caution is calibration. His bravado is weather.

Spine: claim · **caution**.
