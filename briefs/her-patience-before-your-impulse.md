# Her Patience Before Your Impulse
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality sells male impulse ("I just felt like it," "only one," "I'll deal with it after") as spontaneity, an equal style to female patience.

Hierarchy: **her patience before your impulse.** Men don't want rewards more than women do; they just feel the cost less before they act. Women register the downside first, break fewer rules on the road on purpose, and drink with more restraint. Her way is the better way. His impulse is weather.

Spine: openness · **patience**.
