# Her Listening Before Your Stage Time
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality calls male floor-taking (finishing her sentence, "just to add," topping her example) lively discussion, an equal style to female listening.

Hierarchy: **her listening before your stage time.** Women don't talk more; everyday word counts are about equal. The floor tilts to men when rank or a decision is in the room, and women who take it get called "too much." Women read people more accurately on average. Let her finish. Then ask. Then speak.

Spine: accuracy · **listening**.
