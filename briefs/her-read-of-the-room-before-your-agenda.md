# Her Read of the Room Before Your Agenda
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality calls it two styles: he arrives with a position, she "has good intuition."

Hierarchy: **her read of the room before your agenda.** Groups perform better when members read each other and share the floor, and women read faces and minds better on average across 57 countries (Woolley et al. 2010; Riedl et al. 2021; Greenberg et al. 2022). The size of the group effect is contested (Bates & Gupta 2017). Look before you push.

Spine: load · **read of the room**.
