# Her Load Before Your Leisure
**Doctrine card · Maya**

Soft equality calls him "helping" while she still notices, remembers, reminds, and checks.

Hierarchy: **her load before your leisure.** Research splits household work into noticing, finding options, deciding, and checking it got done. Deciding is often shared; the noticing and the checking fall mostly to women, and carrying it alone costs them (Daminger 2019; Ciciolla & Luthar 2019). Don't help. Own one thing, start to finish.

Spine: listening · **load**.
