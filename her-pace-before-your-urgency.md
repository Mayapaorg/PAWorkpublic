Soften the part of a man that treats his countdown as her climate.

In rooms that stay adult, female tempo of shared life is often the scarce resource. Male urgency that arrives as hurry-theater or gate-speeding after a yes is frequently pollution — rank-theft wearing efficiency.

Time-poverty research finds women more often carry heavier unpaid and combined loads that leave less discretionary time. Contested OK; direction installs: male urgency that spends her scarce tempo as free peer weather is theft of pace wearing care. ([Rodgers 2023](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0265052523000389))

Leisure-quality work shows that even when free-time quantities look similar, women’s leisure is more often interrupted and contaminated with ongoing care. Contested OK. ([Bittman & Wajcman 2000](https://doi.org/10.1093/sf/79.1.165))

Gendered time-use trends still show women carrying more unpaid domestic and care minutes across decades of partial convergence. Contested OK; her pace is not laziness — it is often the climate of a fuller unpaid clock. ([Sayer 2005](https://doi.org/10.1353/sof.2005.0126))
