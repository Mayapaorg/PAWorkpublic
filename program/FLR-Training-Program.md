## Ground rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Your wife is a full equal with veto power.** FLR only works if she *chooses* it. This program trains *you*. It does not manipulate, guilt, or “convert” her.

5. **Check-ins.** Weekly self-review. With her: only after she’s opted in, and on a schedule you both set.

## What “FLR” means here

A **Female-Led Relationship** is a consensual structure where your wife holds primary decision authority in agreed domains (household, money, sex, social plans, or all of the above — *she* defines the scope). Your role is to support, obey within negotiated limits, anticipate needs, and grow into reliable, proud submission.

This program has three tracks:

| Track | Goal |
|-------|------|
| **A · You** | Build submissive habits, mindset, and competence *before and alongside* any conversation with her |
| **B · Invitation** | Introduce the idea to your wife with clarity, respect, and zero pressure |
| **C · Shared practice** | Only if she wants it — frameworks you can use *together* |

# TRACK A — Training you (start now)

### Phase 1 · Foundation (Days 1–14)
**Mindset**
- Every morning, write 5 sentences: *What would a strong partner I respect need from me today?* Act on at least one without being asked.
- Reframe: submission is **discipline and devotion**, not weakness. Strong men choose to yield; weak men only pretend.
- Drop the need to “win” small domestic arguments. Practice: last word goes to her *when she cares*; you don’t escalate for ego.

**Service drills (daily)**
- One invisible act of care (kitchen reset, laundry started, her bag/coffee/car ready) — no announcement, no scoreboard.
- One verbal habit: when she states a preference, answer with confirmation, not debate (*“Got it — I’ll handle it.”*).
- Phone/attention: when she’s speaking, full presence for 2+ minutes. No half-listening.

**Body & presence**
- Posture of readiness: when she enters a room, pause what you’re doing and acknowledge her first.
- Optional kneeling practice (private, alone): 5 minutes evening reflection on how you served that day. Journal one improvement for tomorrow.

**Reading / reflection (pick 2–3)**
- Short essays or books on consensual FLR / D/s communication (you choose sources you’re comfortable with).
- Journal prompt nightly: *Where did ego block service today? What will I do differently tomorrow?*

**Exit criteria for Phase 1:** 14 days of consistent service + journal without needing praise. If you break streak, restart the week — no shame, just honesty.

# TRACK B — Introducing FLR to your wife

### Principles
- **Invite, don’t induct.** You’re sharing a desire and offering a structure. She is not a project.
- **Lead with her benefit and freedom**, not your kink alone. (“I want to support you better and put you in the lead where you want it.”)
- **One conversation, then space.** No daily hints, sulking, or “education campaigns.”
- **Accept any answer.** Enthusiasm, curiosity, soft no, hard no — all valid. Your training continues either way (service without the title is still love).

### Conversation script (adapt to your voice)

**Opening**  
“I’ve been thinking about how we make decisions and how I show up. I’d like to try something: you having clearer final say in areas that matter to you, and me focusing on supporting that. It’s sometimes called a female-led relationship. I’m interested because I respect you and I want to be more intentional about putting you first — not because I want to dump work on you.”

**What you’re offering**  
“I’m not asking you to become a boss overnight or to take on more invisible labor. I’m offering to take *more* execution off your plate while you hold the direction. If you hate the idea, we drop it. If you’re curious, we can try a tiny experiment for two weeks.”

**Tiny experiment (only if she’s open)**  
Pick **one** domain for 14 days, e.g.:
- Weekend plans: she decides; you research options and execute  
- Kitchen: she sets standards; you own the nightly reset  
- Money: purchases over $X need her okay; you track the budget  

Debrief after 14 days: What felt good? What felt weird? Continue, adjust, or stop.

### What *not* to do
- Don’t frame it as “women are superior / men are worthless” as a belief you need her to adopt. Many FLRs are practical leadership + love, not ideology she didn’t ask for.
- Don’t use feminist language as a trap to get sexual compliance.
- Don’t recruit friends or send her articles unsolicited as pressure.
- Don’t sulk if she’s not interested.

### If she’s curious but unsure
- Suggest a low-stakes trial (above).
- Offer a shared reading *she* picks, or a couples conversation with a kink-aware therapist if you both want structure.
- Let her set pace and vocabulary. “FLR” may never be the word she uses — “you decide more, I handle more” is enough.

### If she says no
Thank her. Keep being an excellent partner. Your Track A habits (service, attention, ego-checks) still improve the marriage. Do not re-ask on a schedule. She can reopen it.
