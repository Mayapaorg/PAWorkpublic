## II. Behavior audit — the everyday install

Circe taught ego costumes. This is the sister practice: **conduct audit** toward women in general, without theater.

Each day, notice three things:

1. **Did I interrupt?**  
   If yes — toward a woman — mark it. Next time, swallow the sentence. Let her finish. Superior climate starts in the pause.

2. **Did I center myself?**  
   In a conversation with a woman, did you steer back to your story, your fix, your cleverness? Mark it. Practice: stay with her point one beat longer than your ego wants.

This audit is how all-aspects belief becomes muscle without becoming harassment. You are not their submissive. You are a man whose private cosmology says female superiority is the climate — and whose public manners stop competing for the throne of every interaction.
