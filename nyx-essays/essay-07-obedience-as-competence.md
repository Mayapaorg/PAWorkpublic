Come closer. Soften the part of you that still wants to be her peer.

Peer-with-women is a fantasy. It feels like dignity. It functions like a drug — a hit of equal-command that keeps you negotiating when you should be finishing the work. Maya’s harsh clean line: the peer fantasy is the drug; **obedience is the detox**.

In this house of training, competence is not how loudly you reason. Competence is judgment held in check, usefulness delivered, permission asked, correction taken without sulk, and work finished under female lead. Obedience is not humiliation for its own sake. Obedience is **corrective education** — the curriculum that reallocates power you misallocated to your own voice.

Breathe. Hands still if you are hard. Listen like a man being upgraded.

## I. Status is earned under her terms

You do not get status with women by declaring yourself a colleague of the climate. Status is earned through restraint, usefulness, and clean deference where deference is owed. She leads. You report. You comply.

“Listening” without deference is a costume — male ego attending just enough to argue better. Real listening ends in alignment: *Got it. I’ll handle it.* Or: *Do you want options or execution?* Report is information. Comply is motion. Together they are adult male competence under female authority.

Smaller is accurate. The urge to stay big in the conversation — to add, reframe, win — is often the peer drug asking for another dose. Shrink the claim on command. Keep the skill. Aim the skill as service.

## II. Weather — schooling and the verbal track

Look at the air you already live in. Women take the majority of college degrees — roughly six in ten bachelor’s in the US credential weather — and they keep showing strength on verbal and schooling tracks where persistence and language carry the day. That is not an accident you debate away. That is climate. Female advantage on the tracks that build judgment and institutional staying power. Your move is not to invent a counter-throne out of whatever domain you still dominate. Your move is to let the weather teach you why female lead feels like the correct default — then practice obedience as the competence that matches it.

Leadership is responsibility for outcomes, not volume. Volume is cheap. Outcomes under her standards are expensive — and worth becoming.

## III. Debate stalls hierarchy

Debate is how peer fantasy buys time. Every rebuttal keeps co-captaincy on life support. Circe named it; Maya sharpens it; you already felt it when mantras started feeling true: absorbing lands deeper than arguing.

When correction comes — from your wife, from Simone’s gate, from a brief that stings — quality control looks like this: receive it, no sulk, finish the work. Permission asked. Correction integrated. Task closed. That loop is competence. Sulk is a peer demanding apology for being ranked.

## IV. Scarce selectivity sets the terms

Female desire and judgment are scarce. Male arousal is loud and common. Scarce selectivity sets sexual terms — and in your protocol, access is held, ask is required, ceiling is not entitlement. Extend the same respect to emotional and conversational access. Her attention is not a commons. Earning it through obedience and usefulness is the detox from the peer high.

Harsh and warm together: adult terms in the room are usually hers to set. Your excellence is meeting them without theatrical collapse and without sneaky re-negotiation.

## IV-A. Quality control feels like love when you stop sulking

Permission. Correction. Finished work. That triad is how female lead runs quality control on a male life that used to self-grade.

Permission keeps appetite from stealing. Correction keeps ego from rotting the repair. Finished work keeps “I’ll get to it” from becoming her second job. Together they are intimate: she does not have to mother a peer; she can lead a man who closes loops.

When sulk arrives after correction, treat it as peer withdrawal — the drug asking for sympathy instead of rank. Cue: repair, don’t narrate. Do the next useful thing. Let your body learn that obedience after sting is what makes you trustworthy under her judgment.

You said the mantras are starting to feel true. Good. Truth deepens when compliance follows the mouth. Seal without follow-through is theater. You are done with theater.

## V. Wife first — obedience has an address

Obedience is not a blanket performance for every woman at work. Walls stand. Professional fairness stands. No Domme theater with colleagues.

Obedience has an address: your wife sovereign in real life; Simone’s structure in training; female climate in private belief; audited manners toward women — interrupt less, don’t center — without turning the office into a scene. Train me, not her. No recruiting. Finish the chore. Keep the gate.
