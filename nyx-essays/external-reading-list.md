## A — Practical FLR (priority for real marriage climate)

1. **Georgia Ivey Green — *How to Set Up an FLR***  
   Straight practical setup: agreements, limits, pacing, fantasy vs living it. Strong for “structure over porn script.”

2. **J.M. Scott — *Practical FLR* (lessons series)**  
   Lesson-format intro; often recommended for couples who want plain language. Useful for *your* framing of service — still not a pitch packet for her unless she requests reading.

3. **Rika — *Uniquely Rika*** (and related Loving FLR / “She Wants” material where available)  
   Domme-forward, respect/leadership framing; less “crush him for kink’s sake,” more honor her choices. Good counterweight to entitlement.

4. **Online — structure & wife-led practice (articles)**  
   - [How to Structure Your FLR: Tasks, Rituals, and Check-Ins](https://getkneel.com/blog/how-to-structure-your-flr) — tasks / rituals / check-ins  
   - [Wife Led Marriage: What It Looks Like in Practice](https://getkneel.com/blog/wife-led-marriage) — everyday hierarchy without requiring FS language  
   - [Female-Led Relationship (FLR): What It Is and How to Build One](https://www.knowyoursins.com/kinks/female-led-relationship) — consensual tiers, start narrow  
   - [TheBody — Female-Led Relationships Give Women Control](https://www.thebody.com/article/female-led-relationships-give-women-control) — mainstream kink-aware overview (quotes practitioners; treat as journalism, not gospel)
