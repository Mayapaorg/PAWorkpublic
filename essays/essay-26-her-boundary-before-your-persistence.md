Research on social rejection finds that women anticipate more penalties for rejecting, endorse rejection less readily, and that men (not women) rate female rejectors more negatively than male rejectors. Contested OK; direction installs. ([Freedman, Fetterolf & Beer 2019](https://doi.org/10.1080/00224545.2018.1532388))

Negotiation/backlash work shows women adjust assertiveness under threat of social penalty, especially in self-advocacy. Contested OK; usable as doctrine: her stop often costs her more socially than his re-ask costs him. ([Amanatullah & Morris 2010](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0017094); [Bowles, Babcock & Lai 2007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2006.09.001))
