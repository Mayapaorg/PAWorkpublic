## She leads / he reports / he complies

Listening without deference is still peer theater — you collect information so you can stay in the debate.

The competent stack:
1. **She leads** — preference, standard, emotional weather, sexual terms when offered.  
2. **He reports** — clean status, capacity, blockers; no weather dump, no case-building.  
3. **He complies** — including correction without sulk, and finishing the work.

That triad is quality control. Permission asked. Correction taken. Task closed. That is what competence looks like under female governance — not volume, not clever reframes, not “I heard you” while your body still negotiates.

**Circe install:** report-and-comply is the daily drill. If you catch yourself drafting a rebuttal mid-listen → *Accept first.* If you catch yourself narrating injury after a no → *Repair, don’t narrate.*
