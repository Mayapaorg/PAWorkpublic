Soft equality sold male bravado as one equal style among two: his boldness, her carefulness, pick either. Hierarchy names the ranking. **Her caution is calibration. His bravado is weather.**

## The research

Men traded **45% more** than women in a large brokerage dataset, and trading cut men's net returns by **2.65 percentage points a year** against **1.72** for women. The authors' explanation is overconfidence. ([Barber & Odean 2001](https://doi.org/10.1162/003355301556400))

Both sexes were overconfident about their answers, but men were generally more confident, especially when **wrong**. The gap varied by subject. ([Lundeberg, Fox & Punćcohaŕ 1994](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.86.1.114))

With no gender difference in performance, **73%** of men chose a winner-take-all tournament against **35%** of women. Low-performing men entered too much; high-performing women entered too little, so the finding cuts both ways. ([Niederle & Vesterlund 2007](https://doi.org/10.1162/qjec.122.3.1067))

A meta-analysis of 150 studies found men more likely to take risks, with the gap varying by context and age and appearing to shrink over time. ([Byrnes, Miller & Schafer 1999](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.125.3.367))

Male executives made more acquisitions and issued more debt; their acquisitions had announcement returns about **2% lower** than those of female-led firms. Causality is still debated. ([Huang & Kisgen 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2012.12.005))

## Maya's view

Men did more, felt surer, and kept less. That isn't an equal style. It's one instrument that reads the room and one that reads its own reflection. A woman who hesitates is running a check. A man who never hesitates has often skipped the check and called the skip decisiveness.

When a capable woman holds back, give her the room. When an incapable man pushes forward, tell him to sit down. Only one of those mistakes keeps getting called leadership.

The risk gap shrinks with time and context, which means male bravado isn't fate. It's a habit, and habits can be trained down under her caution.
