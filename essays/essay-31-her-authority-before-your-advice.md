## Soft equality pretends male coaching is peer weather

Across **N = 4,394** U.S. women, unsolicited, generic, and prescriptive advice (vs. responsive questions or solicited suggestions) left women feeling less respected, less powerful, and smaller in size of self; when the advice came from a man, women also anticipated greater stereotype threat (**d ≈ 0.42** in the man-coworker contrasts). ([Santoro & Markus 2024](https://doi.org/10.1177/09567976241268630))

Task-group research finds women less influential when they possess expertise — and that having expertise *decreased* how expert others perceived them to be — while groups with a female expert underperformed groups with a male expert. ([Thomas-Hunt & Phillips 2004](https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167204271186))
