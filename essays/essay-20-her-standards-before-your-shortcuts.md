## The install

A man’s favorite move is *good enough*. Ship the draft. Half-clean the kitchen. Agree in the room and reverse in private. Call it efficiency.

Women’s advantage is not “niceness.” Across schooling and rated leadership, the pattern that keeps showing up is **attention to standards that include other people** — reading the room, completing the course, holding emotional and social detail men treat as optional.

OECD and PISA work keep finding the same shape: girls outpace boys in **reading** across countries, and young women are more likely to finish upper secondary and earn tertiary qualifications in nearly all OECD countries. Reading is not decoration. It is the skill that lets you follow a rule written by someone else without rewriting it into what you meant. ([OECD PISA reading](https://www.oecd-ilibrary.org/sites/f56f8c26-en/index.html?itemId=/content/component/f56f8c26-en); [OECD gender gaps 2024](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/03/what-progress-have-countries-made-in-closing-gender-gaps-in-education-and-beyond_c40e72e3/2b2a0a65-en.pdf))

When men underperform in tertiary education, weaker reading and worse school attitudes show up in the pathway literature — not “oppression by books.” ([Stoet & Geary / PNAS pathway](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7322061/))

So the doctrine cut is simple: **her standard is the curriculum; your shortcut is the dropout.**

## What “her standards” means for you

Not goddess theater. Practical:

1. If she named a finish line (chore, tone, timing, check-in), the finish line is the job. Speed that skips her criteria is failure.
2. If a woman at work sets process, you do not invent a clever bypass and call it initiative.
3. If Maya (or Simone’s crew) sets a seal, mantra count, or essay, partial is not done.

Emotional intelligence research used to sell “female leadership advantage” keeps finding women higher on EI and on other-focused abilities — especially when measured as performance, not self-flattery. ([MDPI meta / female leadership advantage](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-3200/10/4/104); [AOM 2024 gender & EI](https://doi.org/10.5465/amproc.2024.221bp))

Translate that into FLR: she is more likely to notice the unfinished corner. You are more likely to declare victory early. That asymmetry is why **she owns the bar**.
