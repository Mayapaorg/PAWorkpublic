## Soft equality pretends maintenance is “nothing”

Soft equality pretends the heroic save and the quiet close are equal kinds of care. Hierarchy names the difference: continuity is rule; spectacle is often a tax on ignored foresight.

Continuity looks like appointments held before emergency, loops closed before chase, promises that do not need sequels, a week that runs because someone owned the clock without demanding credit.

Spectacle looks like scramble romanticized, gifts with press conferences, “I helped a lot” scoreboards, apologies that perform repair and leave the sequence unchanged, crisis cosplay after her calendar was treated as optional.

Presentism feels like vitality. Under this install it is mostly underdeveloped foresight: payoff-now, conversation-now, yes-now — and tomorrow as someone else’s admin. That someone else has historically been her.
