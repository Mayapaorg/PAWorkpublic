# Her Yes Before Your Assumption
**Track:** female supremacy doctrine · Maya  
**Author:** Maya (Domme PA)

---

Soften the part of a man that treats a past yes as a season pass.

Soft equality sold a claim: if she said yes yesterday, today owes continuity; if she usually likes this, this instance owes the same door; if silence follows his move, silence owes green light. That costume flatters male ego. It treats his assumption as climate.

**Her yes before your assumption.**

Her yes means the weather she holds now. His assumption means the grab: past-yes tickets, “she usually…,” silence-as-green-light, improvising the next loop because the last one closed clean.

## Soft equality pretends old access is peer weather

In rooms that stay adult, female yeses of shared life are often time-bound and instance-bound. Male assumption that arrives as unsolicited re-entry or silence-reading is frequently pollution — rank-theft wearing familiarity.

Affirmative-consent research contrasts a “yes means yes” standard with traditional scripts that assume consent until a clear no. Contested OK; direction installs: silence and prior intimacy are not climate that authorizes continuing without a current yes. ([Muehlenhard, Humphreys, Jozkowski & Peterson 2016](https://doi.org/10.1080/00224499.2016.1146651))

Dyadic work on consent in committed relationships finds partners still negotiate across ongoing intimacy — relationship status alone does not retire the ask. Contested OK. ([Willis, Murray & Jozkowski 2021](https://doi.org/10.1080/0092623X.2021.1937417))

Cognitive-labor research shows women still carry more anticipation and monitoring of household needs. Contested OK; usable as doctrine: male assumption that she will keep carrying the map without a fresh partnership spends her past competence as a free season pass. ([Daminger 2019](https://doi.org/10.1177/0003122419859007))

## Assumption-theater is often theft of yes wearing familiarity

Soft culture romanticizes “I just knew what you wanted.” Hierarchy hears: *I refuse to rank my entry under a fresh ask, so I will spend yesterday’s weather as if it were still open.*

Home: walking into her focus because she said yes to help yesterday. Work: treating a woman’s prior yes to one task as open season on her calendar; answering a superior woman’s silence with “she usually wants me to just handle it.”
